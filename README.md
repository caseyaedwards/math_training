# Math Training

A self-paced, picture-first math curriculum, from arithmetic to proof-based higher math.
Every idea comes with a diagram, step-by-step worked examples, and exercises with answers.

## Start Here

1. Skim the [study plan](docs/STUDY_PLAN.md) to pick a pace.
2. Take the [101 checkpoint](course/101/checkpoint.md) cold. If you pass, jump to the next level.
3. Work one lesson per session. Track progress in the [Obsidian vault](obsidian/README.md).

## How a Lesson Works

Every lesson follows the same shape, defined in the [lesson template](docs/LESSON_TEMPLATE.md):

1. **The Big Idea**: two or three plain sentences.
2. **Picture It**: a diagram first, words second.
3. **Step by Step**: the idea built from the simplest case.
4. **Worked Examples**: every line shown, with the reason on the right.
5. **Common Mistakes**, **Try It** exercises with hidden answers, an optional **Code Corner** in Python, and **Go Deeper** links.
