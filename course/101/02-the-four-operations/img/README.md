# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [addition-jumps.svg](addition-jumps.svg): Addition and subtraction as moves on a number line.
* [area-model-23x14.svg](area-model-23x14.svg): Area model splitting 23 by 14 into four rectangles.
* [array-4x3.svg](array-4x3.svg): An array of 4 rows of 3 dots and the same array turned to 3 rows of 4.
* [carry-47-38.svg](carry-47-38.svg): Blocks for 47 plus 38: 7 tens and 15 ones, where 10 ones trade for a ten, giving 85.
* [count-up-62-29.svg](count-up-62-29.svg): Number line counting up from 29 to 30 then to 62, a total of 33.
* [division-missing-side.svg](division-missing-side.svg): Division as finding the missing side of a 72-square rectangle.
* [remainder-17-5.svg](remainder-17-5.svg): Seventeen dots in three groups of five with two left over, and the same shown as jumps of 5 on a number line.
* [sharing-vs-grouping.svg](sharing-vs-grouping.svg): Twelve dots shared into 3 groups of 4, and the same twelve dots grouped in threes making 4 groups.
* [ten-frames-5-plus-4.svg](ten-frames-5-plus-4.svg): Two ten-frames showing 5 + 4 and 4 + 5 both fill 9 cells.
