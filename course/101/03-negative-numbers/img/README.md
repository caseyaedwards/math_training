# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [clock-skew.svg](clock-skew.svg): Number line in milliseconds showing host clocks at -340 and +125, 465 ms apart.
* [negative-number-line.svg](negative-number-line.svg): Number line from -6 to 6 showing 2 + (-5) = -3 and the opposites -4 and 4.
* [sign-grid.svg](sign-grid.svg): Two by two sign table: same signs give plus, different signs give minus.
* [sign-pattern.svg](sign-pattern.svg): Bar chart of -3 times 2, 1, 0, -1, -2 giving -6, -3, 0, 3, 6, rising by 3 each step.
* [subtract-as-add-opposite.svg](subtract-as-add-opposite.svg): Two number lines: 5 minus negative 2 moves right to 7, and negative 4 minus 6 moves left to negative 10.
* [thermometer.svg](thermometer.svg): Vertical thermometer from -10 to 10 with -2 above -7, showing -2 is greater.
* [three-times-minus-four.svg](three-times-minus-four.svg): Three jumps of -4 from 0 landing on -12.
