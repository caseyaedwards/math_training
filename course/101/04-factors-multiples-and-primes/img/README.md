# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [cron-lcm-6-15.svg](cron-lcm-6-15.svg): Timeline of a 6 minute job and a 15 minute job meeting at 0 and 30 minutes.
* [euclid-squares-84-60.svg](euclid-squares-84-60.svg): An 84 by 60 rectangle cut into a 60 square, two 24 squares and two 12 squares; the last square side 12 is the gcd.
* [factor-pairs-36.svg](factor-pairs-36.svg): Factors of 36 in a row with arcs joining pairs that multiply to 36; 6 pairs with itself.
* [factor-rectangles.svg](factor-rectangles.svg): Rectangles of 12 squares (1 by 12, 2 by 6, 3 by 4) and the single 1 by 7 rectangle for 7.
* [factor-tree-84-other-start.svg](factor-tree-84-other-start.svg): Factor tree for 84 starting as 4 times 21, ending in the same primes 2, 2, 3 and 7.
* [factor-tree-84.svg](factor-tree-84.svg): Factor tree for 84 ending in the primes 2, 2, 3 and 7.
* [gcd-lcm-venn.svg](gcd-lcm-venn.svg): Venn diagram of the prime factors of 84 and 60; the overlap 2, 2, 3 gives gcd 12 and all factors give lcm 420.
* [sieve-50.svg](sieve-50.svg): Grid of numbers 1 to 50 with the 15 primes highlighted.
