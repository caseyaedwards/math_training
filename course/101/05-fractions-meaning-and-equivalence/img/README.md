# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [compare-5-8-and-3-5.svg](compare-5-8-and-3-5.svg): Number line from 0 to 1 with eighths above and fifths below; 5/8 is slightly right of 3/5.
* [cut-finer-2-3-to-8-12.svg](cut-finer-2-3-to-8-12.svg): A bar of thirds with 2 shaded; each third cut into 4 gives 8 of 12 shaded, the same length.
* [equal-vs-unequal.svg](equal-vs-unequal.svg): A bar cut into three equal thirds above a bar cut into three unequal pieces, which are not thirds.
* [fraction-bars.svg](fraction-bars.svg): Fraction bars showing 1/2, 2/4, 4/8 and 3/6 cover the same length.
* [fraction-number-line.svg](fraction-number-line.svg): Number line from 0 to 2 in quarters with 3/4 and 5/4 marked.
* [mixed-17-fifths.svg](mixed-17-fifths.svg): Seventeen fifths drawn as three full bars of fifths plus two fifths of a fourth bar.
* [share-3-pizzas-4-ways.svg](share-3-pizzas-4-ways.svg): Three pizzas each cut in quarters for persons A to D; person A collects three quarters.
* [three-eighths-pie-and-bar.svg](three-eighths-pie-and-bar.svg): Three eighths shown as 3 of 8 pie slices and as 3 of 8 bar pieces.
