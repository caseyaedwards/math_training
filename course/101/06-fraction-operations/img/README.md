# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [add-on-number-line.svg](add-on-number-line.svg): Number line in twelfths with a jump of 1/3 then 1/4 landing on 7/12.
* [add-thirds-and-quarters.svg](add-thirds-and-quarters.svg): Bars showing 1/3 + 1/4 re-cut into twelfths to make 7/12.
* [mixed-number-area.svg](mixed-number-area.svg): Area model for 2 1/2 times 1 1/3 split into pieces 2, 1/2, 2/3 and 1/6 totaling 3 1/3.
* [multiply-area.svg](multiply-area.svg): Unit square showing 2/3 times 3/4 as 6 of 12 cells.
* [quarters-in-one-and-a-half.svg](quarters-in-one-and-a-half.svg): A bar one and a half units long cut into six quarters, showing 3/2 divided by 1/4 is 6.
* [same-denominator-2-5-plus-1-5.svg](same-denominator-2-5-plus-1-5.svg): Bars of fifths showing 2/5 plus 1/5 equals 3/5.
* [subtract-5-6-minus-3-8.svg](subtract-5-6-minus-3-8.svg): Five sixths re-cut into 20 twenty-fourths; crossing out 9 leaves 11/24.
* [three-quarters-of-20.svg](three-quarters-of-20.svg): Twenty dots in four columns of five, three columns highlighted to show 3/4 of 20 is 15.
