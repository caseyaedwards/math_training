# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [compare-decimals.svg](compare-decimals.svg): Number line from 0.40 to 0.55 with 0.405, 0.45, 0.5 and 0.54 in order.
* [decimal-columns-4-725.svg](decimal-columns-4-725.svg): Place value columns for 4.725.
* [decimal-zoom.svg](decimal-zoom.svg): Zooming from tenths into hundredths to locate 0.37.
* [eighths-as-decimals.svg](eighths-as-decimals.svg): Number line from 0 to 1 in eighths labeled with fractions above and decimals below; 3/8 is 0.375.
* [error-budget-99-9.svg](error-budget-99-9.svg): A month of 30 day cells with one day zoomed; 43.2 minutes of error budget fills a sliver of that day.
* [hundred-grid-37.svg](hundred-grid-37.svg): Hundred grid with 37 squares shaded showing 0.37 and 37 percent.
* [multiply-1-2-by-0-3.svg](multiply-1-2-by-0-3.svg): Unit square of 100 cells with a 1.2 by 0.3 strip of 36 cells, showing 1.2 times 0.3 is 0.36.
* [percent-change-asymmetry.svg](percent-change-asymmetry.svg): Two bars: 200 growing by 50 is plus 25 percent; 250 shrinking by 50 is minus 20 percent.
* [percent-of-80.svg](percent-of-80.svg): Double number line pairing 0 to 100 percent with 0 to 80, matching 15 percent to 12.
