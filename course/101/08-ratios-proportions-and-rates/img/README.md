# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [cross-multiply-6-9-14-21.svg](cross-multiply-6-9-14-21.svg): The proportion 6/9 = 14/21 with crossing lines showing 6 times 21 and 9 times 14 both equal 126.
* [double-number-line.svg](double-number-line.svg): Double number line pairing seconds with requests at 120 requests per second.
* [equivalent-ratio-groups.svg](equivalent-ratio-groups.svg): One, two and three copies of a group of 3 web hosts and 2 db hosts, showing 3:2, 6:4 and 9:6.
* [part-to-part-and-whole.svg](part-to-part-and-whole.svg): Tape of 5 parts, 3 web and 2 db, contrasting the part to part ratio 3:2 with the part to whole ratio 3:5.
* [pods-for-4000.svg](pods-for-4000.svg): A 4,000 request bar cut into chunks of 187.5; 21 full chunks and a partial 22nd.
* [ratio-graph.svg](ratio-graph.svg): Graph of web hosts against db hosts; the points (2,3), (4,6), (6,9), (8,12) lie on one line through the origin.
* [scale-factor-3-4-15-20.svg](scale-factor-3-4-15-20.svg): The proportion 3/4 = 15/20 with times 5 arrows on the tops and the bottoms.
* [tape-diagram.svg](tape-diagram.svg): Tape diagram splitting 25 hosts in the ratio 3 to 2.
* [unit-conversion-chain.svg](unit-conversion-chain.svg): Conversion chain from 6 GB per minute to 6,000 MB per minute to 100 MB per second.
