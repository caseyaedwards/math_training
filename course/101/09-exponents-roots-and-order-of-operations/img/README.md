# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [bracket-root-50.svg](bracket-root-50.svg): Paired lines: squares from 49 to 64 above, sides from 7 to 8 below; 50 maps to about 7.07.
* [doubling-tree.svg](doubling-tree.svg): A binary tree splitting in two at each of three levels, ending in 8 leaves.
* [expression-tree.svg](expression-tree.svg): Expression tree for 3 + 4 times 2 squared evaluating to 19.
* [order-of-operations-ladder.svg](order-of-operations-ladder.svg): Four-step ladder: parentheses, exponents, multiplication and division left to right, addition and subtraction left to right.
* [powers-of-minus-two.svg](powers-of-minus-two.svg): Bars for -2 to the 0 through 5: 1, -2, 4, -8, 16, -32 alternating above and below zero.
* [powers-of-two-bars.svg](powers-of-two-bars.svg): Bars of length 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 for 2 to the 0 through 2 to the 5, each double the one before.
* [product-rule-2-3-times-2-4.svg](product-rule-2-3-times-2-4.svg): Three boxes of 2 times four boxes of 2 regrouped as seven boxes of 2.
* [sign-trap-trees.svg](sign-trap-trees.svg): Two expression trees: minus applied after 3 squared gives -9; -3 squared in parentheses gives 9.
* [square-numbers.svg](square-numbers.svg): Squares of side 1 to 4 showing square numbers and square roots.
