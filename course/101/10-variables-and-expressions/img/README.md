# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed.

* [anatomy-of-an-expression.svg](anatomy-of-an-expression.svg): The expression 5x squared minus 3x plus 7 labeled with coefficient, exponent, term and constant.
* [bags-3x-plus-2.svg](bags-3x-plus-2.svg): Three bags labeled x and two loose dots for 3x + 2; below, each bag holds 4 dots, giving 14.
* [distributive-area.svg](distributive-area.svg): Rectangle 3 tall and x plus 2 wide split into areas 3x and 6.
* [expression-vs-equation.svg](expression-vs-equation.svg): Left: the expression 3x + 2 alone in a box. Right: a balance scale with 3x + 2 on one pan and 14 on the other.
* [factoring-12x-plus-18.svg](factoring-12x-plus-18.svg): Rectangle of height 6 split into widths 2x and 3 with areas 12x and 18, showing 12x + 18 = 6(2x + 3).
* [function-machine.svg](function-machine.svg): Pipeline showing x equals 4 going through times 3 then plus 2 to give 14.
* [like-and-unlike-terms.svg](like-and-unlike-terms.svg): Three bags plus five bags make eight bags; below, a strip x and a square x squared cannot combine.
* [table-and-plot-3x-plus-2.svg](table-and-plot-3x-plus-2.svg): Table of x from -2 to 2 with values of 3x + 2, and the same five points plotted on a grid.
