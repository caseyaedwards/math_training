# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [bases.svg](bases.svg): Exponential curves with bases 1.5, 2, e and 3, all through (0, 1).
* [compounding-e.svg](compounding-e.svg): Compounding more often: (1 + 1/n)^n climbs toward e = 2.71828 as n grows.
* [exponent-rules.svg](exponent-rules.svg): Product rule as counting factors: 2^3 times 2^2 is five 2s multiplied, which is 2^5.
* [exponential-backoff.svg](exponential-backoff.svg): Exponential backoff: retry waits of 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 seconds on a timeline.
* [growth-and-decay.svg](growth-and-decay.svg): Exponential growth 2^x and decay (1/2)^x, both through (0, 1).
* [half-life.svg](half-life.svg): Half-life decay: 200 mg of caffeine halves every 5 hours.
* [linear-vs-exponential.svg](linear-vs-exponential.svg): Linear 4x against exponential 2^x: the exponential wins after x = 4.
* [powers-staircase.svg](powers-staircase.svg): Walking down the powers of 2 by dividing by 2: 8, 4, 2, 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, which explains zero and negative exponents.
