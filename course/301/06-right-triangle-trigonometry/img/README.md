# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [elevation-example.svg](elevation-example.svg): Angle of elevation example: standing 40 m from a tower, the top is 35 degrees up, so the tower is 40 tan 35 = 28.0 m tall.
* [labels-depend-on-angle.svg](labels-depend-on-angle.svg): The same triangle labeled from angle A and from angle B: opposite and adjacent swap, the hypotenuse stays.
* [ladder-example.svg](ladder-example.svg): Ladder example: a 5 m ladder reaches 4 m up a wall, so sin theta = 4/5 and theta is about 53.1 degrees.
* [pythagoras-squares.svg](pythagoras-squares.svg): Pythagorean theorem: on a 3-4-5 right triangle the squares on the legs, 9 and 16, add to the square on the hypotenuse, 25.
* [similar-triangles.svg](similar-triangles.svg): Three similar right triangles with the same 35 degree angle: the ratio opposite over hypotenuse is 0.574 in all three.
* [soh-cah-toa.svg](soh-cah-toa.svg): Right triangle with angle theta: opposite, adjacent and hypotenuse.
* [special-triangles.svg](special-triangles.svg): The two special right triangles: 45-45-90 with sides 1, 1, root 2 and 30-60-90 with sides 1, root 3, 2.
