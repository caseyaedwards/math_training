# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [arc-length.svg](arc-length.svg): Arc length: radius 10 cm and angle 1.5 radians give an arc of 15 cm.
* [degrees-radians-lines.svg](degrees-radians-lines.svg): Double number line converting degrees to radians: 0 to 360 degrees lines up with 0 to 2 pi.
* [quadrant-signs.svg](quadrant-signs.svg): Signs of sine, cosine and tangent in the four quadrants.
* [radian.svg](radian.svg): One radian: the angle whose arc is exactly one radius long.
* [reference-angle.svg](reference-angle.svg): Reference angle: 150 degrees sits 30 degrees short of the negative x-axis, so its point mirrors the 30 degree point.
* [unit-circle-triangle.svg](unit-circle-triangle.svg): Unit circle point at 30 degrees: the triangle has hypotenuse 1, so the point is (cos 30, sin 30) = (root 3 over 2, 1/2).
* [unit-circle.svg](unit-circle.svg): The unit circle with the special angles in degrees and radians and the first-quadrant coordinates.
