# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [amplitude-period.svg](amplitude-period.svg): The curve y = 2 sin 2x has amplitude 2 and period pi, compared with y = sin x.
* [daily-traffic.svg](daily-traffic.svg): Daily traffic model R(t) = 500 + 300 sin(2 pi (t - 6) / 24): midline 500, amplitude 300, period 24 hours.
* [even-odd.svg](even-odd.svg): Even and odd: the points at theta and minus theta share x, so cos is even, and have opposite y, so sin is odd.
* [phase-shift.svg](phase-shift.svg): Phase shift: y = sin(x - pi/3) is y = sin x moved right by pi/3.
* [pythagorean-identity.svg](pythagorean-identity.svg): Point on the unit circle at angle theta with legs cos theta and sin theta, giving cos squared plus sin squared equals 1.
* [sin-cos-graphs.svg](sin-cos-graphs.svg): Graphs of sine and cosine from minus 2 pi to 2 pi.
* [tan-graph.svg](tan-graph.svg): Graph of tan x from minus 3 pi over 2 to 3 pi over 2 with vertical asymptotes at odd multiples of pi over 2.
* [unwrap-sine.svg](unwrap-sine.svg): Unwrapping the unit circle: the height of the point becomes the sine graph.
