# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [ambiguous-case.svg](ambiguous-case.svg): The ambiguous case SSA: angle A = 30 degrees, b = 10, a = 6 gives two different triangles.
* [cosines-vs-pythagoras.svg](cosines-vs-pythagoras.svg): Law of cosines extends Pythagoras: with sides 4 and 4, an angle of 60, 90 or 120 degrees makes c squared less than, equal to or greater than 32.
* [law-of-cosines-example.svg](law-of-cosines-example.svg): Law of cosines example: sides 5 and 8 with a 60 degree angle between them give a third side of 7.
* [law-of-sines-example.svg](law-of-sines-example.svg): Triangulating a fire: towers 10 km apart see the fire at 50 and 60 degrees from the baseline.
* [oblique-triangle.svg](oblique-triangle.svg): Triangle ABC with sides a, b, c opposite the matching angles and height h from C.
* [triangle-area.svg](triangle-area.svg): Triangle area one half a b sin C: the height over side a is b sin C.
* [which-law.svg](which-law.svg): Which law to use: SAS and SSS use the law of cosines; ASA and AAS use the law of sines; SSA uses the law of sines and gives zero, one or two triangles.
