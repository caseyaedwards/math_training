# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [arithmetic-vs-geometric.svg](arithmetic-vs-geometric.svg): Arithmetic sequence 3, 5, 7, ... grows by adding 2; geometric sequence 1, 2, 4, ... grows by doubling.
* [gauss-staircase.svg](gauss-staircase.svg): Two staircases of 1 to 6 blocks fit together into a 6 by 7 rectangle, so 1 + 2 + ... + 6 = 6 times 7 over 2 = 21.
* [halving-square.svg](halving-square.svg): A unit square cut in halves again and again: 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + ... fills the whole square.
* [odd-squares.svg](odd-squares.svg): Odd numbers build squares: 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 25 = 5 squared, each odd number an L-shaped layer.
* [partial-sums.svg](partial-sums.svg): Partial sums: the geometric series 1/2 + 1/4 + ... levels off at 1, while the harmonic series 1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + ... keeps climbing.
* [sigma-anatomy.svg](sigma-anatomy.svg): Anatomy of sigma notation: the sum from k = 1 to 5 of 2k - 1.
