# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for the lesson in the parent folder. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [approach-number-lines.svg](approach-number-lines.svg): As x approaches 2 from both sides, x squared approaches 4.
* [rectangles-to-area.svg](rectangles-to-area.svg): Area under y = x squared from 0 to 1 with 4 and 16 rectangles: 0.219 and 0.303, closing in on 1/3.
* [secant-to-tangent.svg](secant-to-tangent.svg): Secant lines through (1, 1) on y = x squared approach the tangent line with slope 2.
* [sinx-over-x.svg](sinx-over-x.svg): Graph of sin x over x with an open circle at (0, 1): the value is undefined but the limit is 1.
* [three-limit-cases.svg](three-limit-cases.svg): Three cases at x = 2: continuous, a hole with a misplaced dot, and a jump with no limit.
* [tolerance-band.svg](tolerance-band.svg): Limit at infinity: (2x + 1)/(x - 3) enters the band 2 plus or minus 0.25 for good once x passes 31.
