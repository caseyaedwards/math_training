# Figures for Limits and Continuity

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Limits and Continuity](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [hole-approach.svg](hole-approach.svg): The line y equals x plus 1 with an open circle (a hole) at the point (1, 2). Orange dots on the left at x equals 0.5, 0.8, 0.9, 0.95 and gray dots on the right at 1.5, 1.2, 1.1, 1.05 crowd toward the hole. Arrows on the x-axis point toward 1 from both sides.
* [one-sided-limits.svg](one-sided-limits.svg): A graph in two pieces. The left piece rises to an open circle at height 1.5 above x equals 2. The right piece starts at a filled dot at height 3 and slopes gently down. An orange arrow approaches 1.5 from the left, a gray arrow approaches 3 from the right.
* [epsilon-band.svg](epsilon-band.svg): Graph of y equals x squared over 4 plus 1. A horizontal orange band of half-height epsilon surrounds the height L equals 2. A vertical gray band of half-width delta surrounds a equals 2. Inside the gray window, the curve, highlighted in orange, stays inside the orange band.
* [two-number-lines.svg](two-number-lines.svg): Two horizontal number lines. The top one is x, with a gray window from 1.6 to 2.4. The bottom one is f of x, with an orange band from 1.5 to 2.5. Arrows from five points in the top window land inside the bottom band.
* [discontinuities.svg](discontinuities.svg): Three small graphs. Left: a line with an open circle at x equals 1 and a separate orange dot above it, a removable discontinuity. Middle: a step that jumps from height 1 to height 3 at x equals 1. Right: a curve that shoots up to infinity on both sides of a dashed vertical line at x equals 1.
* [horizontal-asymptote.svg](horizontal-asymptote.svg): Graph of 3 x squared plus 1 over x squared plus 5 from x equals 0 to 20. It starts near 0.2, rises quickly, and flattens out just under a dashed orange line at y equals 3.
* [ivt-bisection.svg](ivt-bisection.svg): Graph of x cubed plus x minus 1, below the axis at x equals 0 (value negative 1) and above at x equals 1 (value 1). An orange dot marks the crossing near 0.6823. Underneath, five orange bars show the interval shrinking: 0 to 1, 0.5 to 1, 0.5 to 0.75, 0.625 to 0.75, 0.625 to 0.6875.
* [sin-over-x-circle.svg](sin-over-x-circle.svg): A quarter of a unit circle with an angle x of 0.45 radians at the center. The orange arc along the circle has length x. A dark vertical segment from the arc down to the radius has height sine x. The two are nearly the same length.
* [sin-over-x-graph.svg](sin-over-x-graph.svg): Graph of sine x over x from negative 10 to 10. A tall central hump reaches up to an open orange circle at (0, 1), with smaller ripples dying out on both sides.
