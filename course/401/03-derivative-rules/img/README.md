# Figures for Derivative Rules

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Derivative Rules](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [power-rule-square.svg](power-rule-square.svg): A gray square labeled x squared with side x. Two orange strips, each x by dx, are attached on the right and bottom. A tiny dark corner square dx by dx fills the remaining gap.
* [power-rule-graph.svg](power-rule-graph.svg): Two stacked graphs. Top: the curve x cubed with short orange tangent lines at x equals negative 1, 0, 1, and 1.4. Bottom: the orange parabola 3 x squared with dots at heights 3, 0, 3, and 5.88 directly below.
* [product-rule-rectangle.svg](product-rule-rectangle.svg): A gray rectangle labeled f times g. An orange strip g times df is attached on the right and an orange strip f times dg along the bottom. A tiny dark corner df times dg fills the gap.
* [quotient-as-product.svg](quotient-as-product.svg): Four boxes connected by arrows: f over g; rewritten as f times g to the minus 1; product rule plus chain rule gives f prime g to the minus 1 plus f times negative g to the minus 2 times g prime; over a common denominator this is f prime g minus f g prime over g squared.
* [chain-rule-pipeline.svg](chain-rule-pipeline.svg): Three boxes in a row: x, then u equals g of x, then y equals f of u. The first arrow is labeled du over dx equals 3 and the second dy over du equals 2. An orange arrow underneath reads dy over dx equals 2 times 3 equals 6.
* [chain-rule-gears.svg](chain-rule-gears.svg): Three meshed gears of decreasing size. The big gray gear x turns once, the middle orange gear u turns 3 times, and the small dark gear y turns 6 times. Text: du over dx equals 3, dy over du equals 2, dy over dx equals 3 times 2 equals 6.
* [exp-slope.svg](exp-slope.svg): Graph of e to the x with short orange tangent lines at x equals negative 1, 0, 1, and 2. Labels say height 0.37 equals slope, height 1.00 equals slope, height 2.72 equals slope, height 7.39 equals slope.
* [sin-cos-slopes.svg](sin-cos-slopes.svg): Two stacked graphs from 0 to 2 pi. Top: sine x with short orange tangent lines at 0, pi over 2, pi, 3 pi over 2, and 2 pi. Bottom: the orange cosine curve with dots at heights 1, 0, negative 1, 0, 1 directly below.
