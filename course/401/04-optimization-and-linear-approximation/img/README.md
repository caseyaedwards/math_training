# Figures for Optimization and Linear Approximation

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Optimization and Linear Approximation](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [fence-optimization.svg](fence-optimization.svg): Graph of area A of x equals x times 20 minus x for x from 0 to 20, a hill shape. A flat orange tangent touches the peak at x equals 10, A equals 100. Gray tangent lines at x equals 4 and 16 slope up and down, labeled A prime greater than 0 and A prime less than 0.
* [critical-points.svg](critical-points.svg): Graph of x cubed minus 6 x squared plus 9x plus 1 with a flat orange tangent at the local max (1, 5) and another at the local min (3, 1). A sign row underneath shows f prime is plus (rising) before 1, minus (falling) between 1 and 3, and plus (rising) after 3.
* [cup-cap.svg](cup-cap.svg): Three small graphs, each with a flat orange tangent at the origin. Left: a downward cap, f double prime less than 0, a maximum. Middle: an upward cup, f double prime greater than 0, a minimum. Right: x cubed, f double prime equals 0, neither a max nor a min.
* [closed-interval.svg](closed-interval.svg): Graph of x cubed minus 3x with the interval from 0 to 2 shaded. Orange dots mark the left endpoint (value 0), the critical point at x equals 1 (value negative 2, the minimum), and the right endpoint at x equals 2 (value 2, the maximum).
* [fence-rectangles.svg](fence-rectangles.svg): Three rectangles drawn to scale, each with 40 m of fence: 16 m by 4 m with area 64 square metres, 10 m by 10 m with area 100 square metres highlighted in orange, and 18 m by 2 m with area 36 square metres.
* [linear-approximation.svg](linear-approximation.svg): Graph of y equals square root of x from 0 to 9 with an orange tangent line at the point (4, 2). The line is L of x equals 2 plus x minus 4 over 4. Near x equals 4 the line and curve almost coincide. Far away they separate.
* [differential.svg](differential.svg): Graph of y equals x squared near x equals 1 with an orange tangent line. From x equals 1 to 1.6 (dx equals 0.6), the tangent rises dy equals 1.2 (orange segment) while the curve rises the true delta y equals 1.56. The gap 0.36 is dx squared.
* [newton-steps.svg](newton-steps.svg): Graph of x squared minus 2. Starting at x equals 1 (below the axis), an orange tangent line runs up to the axis at x equals 1.5. From the curve above 1.5, a second tangent runs down to the axis at about 1.4167, very close to the true root.
* [open-box.svg](open-box.svg): Left: a 12 by 12 sheet with orange squares of side x cut from each corner and dashed fold lines enclosing a middle square of side 12 minus 2x. Right: the folded open box drawn in 3D, 8 by 8 by 2, volume 128 cubic centimetres.
* [batch-cost.svg](batch-cost.svg): Graph of cost per record against batch size b. A dashed curve 50 over b falls, a dotted line 0.02 b rises, and their sum, a solid U-shaped curve, bottoms out at b equals 50 with cost 2 ms, marked with a flat orange tangent.
