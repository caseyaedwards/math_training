# Figures for Related Rates

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Related Rates](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [ladder.svg](ladder.svg): A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall. Its base is 6 m from the wall and its top is 8 m up. An orange arrow at the base points away from the wall, labeled dx over dt equals 1 m per second. An orange arrow at the top points down, labeled dy over dt equals question mark. The equation x squared plus y squared equals 10 squared holds at every instant.
* [ripple-ring.svg](ripple-ring.svg): A gray disk of radius r with an orange ring of thickness dr around it. The ring is labeled: circumference times thickness equals 2 pi r times dr. Below: dA over dt equals 2 pi r times dr over dt.
* [water-cone.svg](water-cone.svg): An upside-down cone 6 m tall with a top radius of 3 m. Orange water fills it to height h equals 4 m, with water-surface radius r. An arrow at the top shows 2 cubic metres per minute pouring in.
* [ladder-speed.svg](ladder-speed.svg): Graph of the falling speed of the ladder top against the base distance x, with the base moving at 1 m per second. The curve starts near 0, reaches 0.75 m per second at x equals 6 and 1.33 at x equals 8, then shoots upward as x approaches 10.
* [queue-tank.svg](queue-tank.svg): A tank labeled queue Q of t, partly filled with orange and holding six small boxes for requests. An orange arrow on the left brings arrivals at 120 requests per second. A dark arrow on the right removes served requests at 100 per second. Below: dQ over dt equals lambda minus mu equals 20 requests per second, piling up.
* [mesh-links.svg](mesh-links.svg): Three complete graphs with 4, 5, and 6 nodes, every pair joined by an orange line. Labels: 6 links, 10 links, 15 links. Below: links L equals n times n minus 1 over 2, and dL over dt equals 2n minus 1 over 2 times dn over dt.
