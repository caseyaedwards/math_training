# Figures for Riemann Sums and Area

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Riemann Sums and Area](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [riemann-left-right.svg](riemann-left-right.svg): Two side-by-side graphs of y equals x squared from 0 to 2, each with four orange rectangles of width 0.5. On the left, each rectangle takes its height from its left edge and sits under the curve: total 1.75. On the right, heights come from right edges and poke above the curve: total 3.75.
* [riemann-one-strip.svg](riemann-one-strip.svg): A curve over an interval from a to b, divided into six orange strips. One strip is darker: its width is labeled delta x and its height f of x sub i.
* [midpoint-trapezoid.svg](midpoint-trapezoid.svg): Two graphs of y equals x squared from 0 to 2 with four strips each. Left panel: midpoint rectangles, each touching the curve at the middle of its top, total 2.625. Right panel: trapezoids whose slanted tops follow the curve closely, total 2.75.
* [riemann-refine.svg](riemann-refine.svg): Three graphs of y equals x squared from 0 to 2 with left-endpoint rectangles: 4 strips give 1.75, 8 strips give 2.1875, 16 strips give 2.4219. The gaps under the curve shrink each time.
* [signed-area.svg](signed-area.svg): Graph of sine x from 0 to 2 pi. The hump above the axis from 0 to pi is orange and labeled plus 2. The dip below the axis from pi to 2 pi is gray and labeled minus 2. The net integral is 0.
* [rate-buckets.svg](rate-buckets.svg): A bar chart of request rate per minute over six minutes: 120, 180, 260, 300, 220, and 140 requests per second. Inside each bar is its area in requests: 7,200, 10,800, 15,600, 18,000, 13,200, 8,400. The total is 73,200 requests.
