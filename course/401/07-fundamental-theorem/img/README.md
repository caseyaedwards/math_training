# Figures for The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [ftc-two-panels.svg](ftc-two-panels.svg): Two stacked graphs. Top: a rate curve f of t equals 2 plus sine t, shaded orange from 0 up to x equals 3.5, where the shaded area is 8.94 and the curve height is 1.65. Bottom: the accumulated total A of x equals 2x plus 1 minus cosine x, with an orange dot at x equals 3.5 at height 8.94 and an orange tangent of slope 1.65.
* [accumulation-strip.svg](accumulation-strip.svg): The rate curve 2 plus sine t with the region from 0 to x shaded light orange and labeled A of x. A thin darker strip from x to x plus h is labeled strip approximately f of x times h.
* [antiderivative-family.svg](antiderivative-family.svg): Five parallel parabolas x squared plus C for C from negative 2 to 2, with the C equals 0 curve in orange. At x equals 1, each curve has a short tangent line, and all tangents are parallel with slope 2.
* [ftc-evaluate.svg](ftc-evaluate.svg): Three small graphs of y equals x squared. The first shades 0 to 3 and is labeled F of 3 equals 9. The second shades 0 to 1 and is labeled minus F of 1 equals one third. The third shades 1 to 3 and is labeled equals 26 over 3.
* [counter-increase.svg](counter-increase.svg): A request rate curve over 60 seconds with the region between a equals 10 and b equals 40 shaded orange and labeled area approximately 6,955 requests. Caption: increase of N over a to b equals N of b minus N of a equals the integral from a to b of N prime of t.
