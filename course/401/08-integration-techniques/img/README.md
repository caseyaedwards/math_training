# Figures for Integration Techniques

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Integration Techniques](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [by-parts-areas.svg](by-parts-areas.svg): A curve in the u-v plane from the point (u1, v1) to (u2, v2). The orange region under the curve, between u1 and u2, is labeled integral of v du. The gray region to the left of the curve, between v1 and v2, is labeled integral of u dv. A small white rectangle u1 v1 sits in the corner. Together the regions fill the big rectangle u2 v2 minus the small one.
* [substitution-ruler.svg](substitution-ruler.svg): Two number lines. The top one is x with equal steps of 0.25 up to square root of pi over 2. The bottom one is u equals x squared. Dashed lines connect each x tick to its u value: the u ticks start crowded near 0 and spread apart. The last tick maps square root of pi over 2 to pi over 2.
* [substitution-areas.svg](substitution-areas.svg): Two graphs side by side. Left: y equals 2x cosine x squared from 0 to square root of pi over 2, a hump shaded orange with area 1. Right: y equals cosine u from 0 to pi over 2, a falling curve shaded orange with area 1.
* [exp-mean.svg](exp-mean.svg): Graph over t from 0 to 8. A dashed gray curve e to the minus t falls from 1. A solid curve t times e to the minus t rises to a peak at t equals 1 and then decays; the region under it is shaded orange and labeled area equals 1.
