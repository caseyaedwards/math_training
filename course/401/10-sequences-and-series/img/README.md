# Figures for Sequences and Series

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Sequences and Series](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [geometric-squares.svg](geometric-squares.svg): A unit square cut into pieces: the left half labeled one half, then the top half of what remains labeled one quarter, then half of the rest labeled one eighth, then 1/16, 1/32, 1/64, each piece half of what is left, spiralling into the bottom right corner. The pieces darken as they shrink.
* [sequence-band.svg](sequence-band.svg): Dots for a sub n equals 1 plus negative 1 to the n over n, for n from 1 to 15. The first few bounce widely: 0, 1.5, 0.67, 1.25, 0.8. An orange band from 0.8 to 1.2 surrounds the height 1. After n equals 5, every dot (in orange) stays inside the band, alternating above and below 1.
* [geo-number-line.svg](geo-number-line.svg): A number line from 0 to 1. Orange arcs jump from 0 to 1/2, then 1/2 to 3/4, then 3/4 to 7/8, each jump half as long as the one before, piling up just short of 1.
* [partial-sums.svg](partial-sums.svg): Partial sums plotted for n from 1 to 30. Orange dots for 1 plus a half plus a third and so on climb steadily past 3.9 with no sign of stopping. Dark dots for 1 plus a quarter plus a ninth and so on flatten out just under a dashed line at pi squared over 6, about 1.645.
* [harmonic-integral.svg](harmonic-integral.svg): Orange bars of width 1 and height 1/n for n from 1 to 9 stand on the x-axis, each starting at x equals n. The curve y equals 1 over x runs below their tops. The bars contain more area than the region under the curve.
* [alternating.svg](alternating.svg): Partial sums of 1 minus a half plus a third minus a quarter and so on, for n from 1 to 20. The dots zigzag above and below a dashed orange line at ln 2, about 0.6931, with the swings shrinking each step.
* [backoff.svg](backoff.svg): A timeline of retries with waits of 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32 seconds, each block darker than the last. Tick marks at 0, 7, 15, 31, and 63 seconds. Caption: total after n retries is 2 to the n minus 1 seconds; six retries take 63 seconds.
