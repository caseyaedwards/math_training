# Figures for Taylor Series

Hand-authored SVG figures for the [Taylor Series](../README.md) lesson, in the order they appear. Each figure has a generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [taylor-sin.svg](taylor-sin.svg): Graph of sine x from negative 7 to 7 in dark, with four orange Taylor polynomials: degree 1 (dotted line y equals x), degree 3, degree 5, and degree 7 (solid). Each higher degree follows the sine wave further from 0 before peeling away.
* [taylor-exp.svg](taylor-exp.svg): Graph of e to the x in dark from negative 3 to 2.5, with orange Taylor polynomials of degree 1 (a line), degree 2 (a parabola), and degree 3. All pass through (0, 1). The higher the degree, the longer it follows the exponential curve on both sides.
* [radius-of-convergence.svg](radius-of-convergence.svg): Graph of 1 over 1 minus x in dark, shooting up near x equals 1. Gray dashed partial sums 1 plus x plus up to x to the n, for n equals 2, 4, 8. Inside the shaded band from negative 1 to 1 they close in on the curve. Outside it, they veer away.
* [availability-approx.svg](availability-approx.svg): Graph against p from 0 to 0.02. A dark curve 1 minus 1 minus p to the 100 rises and bends over toward 1. An orange dashed line 100p starts on top of the curve and then climbs straight past it. At p equals 0.001 the exact value is 0.0952 and the estimate is 0.1.
