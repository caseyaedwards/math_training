# Diagrams: Counting: Permutations, Combinations and the Pigeonhole Principle

Hand-authored SVG figures used by [the lesson](../README.md). Each file has a matching generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [counting-tree.svg](counting-tree.svg): Tree of choices for arranging A, B, C: 3 branches, then 2, then 1, giving 6 leaves ABC to CBA.
* [lattice-paths.svg](lattice-paths.svg): 3 by 2 grid with two example paths from bottom-left to top-right, RRRUU and URRUR; there are C(5,2) = 10 such paths.
* [perm-vs-comb.svg](perm-vs-comb.svg): Six ordered strings ABC, ACB, BAC, BCA, CAB, CBA with arrows all pointing to the single set {A, B, C}.
* [pigeonhole.svg](pigeonhole.svg): Five dots placed in four boxes; box 1 holds two dots, illustrating the pigeonhole principle.
* [product-rule-grid.svg](product-rule-grid.svg): Grid of three regions by two instance sizes giving six cells, illustrating the product rule.
* [slots.svg](slots.svg): Three slot boxes labeled primary, secondary, tertiary holding 10, 9 and 8 choices, multiplying to 720; dividing by 6 gives 120 groups.
