# Diagrams: Probability Basics

Hand-authored SVG figures used by [the lesson](../README.md). Each file has a matching generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [addition-rule.svg](addition-rule.svg): Venn diagram of hearts (13) and kings (4) overlapping in one card, the king of hearts; regions hold 12, 1 and 3 cards.
* [dice-complement.svg](dice-complement.svg): Dice grid with the last row and column shaded: 11 cells contain a six, and the unshaded 5 by 5 block of 25 cells has no six.
* [dice-grid.svg](dice-grid.svg): Six by six grid of two-dice outcomes showing each sum; the six cells with sum 7 form the highlighted diagonal.
* [independence-area.svg](independence-area.svg): Unit square cut vertically at 0.3 for A and horizontally at 0.5 for B; the corner rectangle A and B has area 0.15.
* [series-parallel.svg](series-parallel.svg): Top: client calls X then Y in series, availability 0.999 times 0.999 = 0.998001. Bottom: three replicas of 0.99 in parallel, availability 0.999999.
