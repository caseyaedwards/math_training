# Diagrams: Conditional Probability and Bayes

Hand-authored SVG figures used by [the lesson](../README.md). Each file has a matching generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [bayes-area.svg](bayes-area.svg): Unit square split 10 percent bad and 90 percent good; alert region shaded; bad-and-alert strip is one third of the shaded area.
* [bayes-tree.svg](bayes-tree.svg): Probability tree: bad deploy 0.1 then alert 0.9, good deploy 0.9 then alert 0.2; alert leaves 0.09 and 0.18 highlighted.
* [conditional-zoom.svg](conditional-zoom.svg): Dice grid with all cells greyed except the five with sum 8; the cell first die 3, second die 5 is dark: one fifth of the new sample space.
* [icon-array.svg](icon-array.svg): Grid of 100 squares: the left column of 10 bad deploys has 9 dark alerted squares; the 90 good deploys include 18 light alerted squares; 9 of 27 alerts are bad.
* [odds-update.svg](odds-update.svg): Two stacked bars: prior odds bad to good 1 to 9; after multiplying the bad part by 4.5 the odds are 4.5 to 9, that is 1 to 2.
