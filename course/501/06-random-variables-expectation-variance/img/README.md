# Diagrams: Random Variables, Expectation and Variance

Hand-authored SVG figures used by [the lesson](../README.md). Each file has a matching generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [latency-mean.svg](latency-mean.svg): Two bars: probability 0.9 at 20 ms and 0.1 at 200 ms; a dashed line at the mean of 38 ms lies where no bar is.
* [linearity.svg](linearity.svg): Three boxes: expected value of die 1 is 3.5, plus die 2 is 3.5, equals expected sum 7.
* [pmf-two-dice.svg](pmf-two-dice.svg): Bar chart of the sum of two dice from 2 to 12, heights 1/36 to 6/36 to 1/36, balanced on a fulcrum at 7.
* [spread-compare.svg](spread-compare.svg): Two distributions with mean 5: values 4, 5, 6 close together with variance 2/3, and values 1, 5, 9 far apart with variance 32/3.
* [variance-squares.svg](variance-squares.svg): Two number lines with mean 5. Values 4, 5, 6 have tiny squares of area 1 on their distances; values 1, 5, 9 have big squares of area 16.
