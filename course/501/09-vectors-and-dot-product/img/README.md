# Diagrams: Vectors and the Dot Product

Hand-authored SVG figures used by [the lesson](../README.md). Each file has a matching generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [dot-projection.svg](dot-projection.svg): Vectors a = (4,1) and b = (2,3) with angle theta; the shadow (projection) of b onto a is highlighted.
* [dot-sign.svg](dot-sign.svg): Three panels: acute angle gives positive dot product, right angle gives zero, obtuse angle gives negative.
* [scalar-multiple.svg](scalar-multiple.svg): Vector v = (2,1) with its multiples 2v = (4,2), 0.5v = (1,0.5) and -v = (-2,-1), all on one line through the origin.
* [vector-add.svg](vector-add.svg): Vector addition on a grid: u = (3,1) and v = (1,3) placed tip to tail reach u + v = (4,4), forming a parallelogram.
* [vector-components.svg](vector-components.svg): Vector (3,4) drawn as an arrow from the origin with dashed legs 3 across and 4 up; its length is 5 by Pythagoras.
