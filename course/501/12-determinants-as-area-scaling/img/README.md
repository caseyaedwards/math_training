# Diagrams: Determinants as Area Scaling

Hand-authored SVG figures used by [the lesson](../README.md). Each file has a matching generator spec comment above its embed in the lesson.

* [det-any-shape.svg](det-any-shape.svg): A small circle, square and triangle before the matrix, and their images after it: each image has 5 times the original area.
* [det-area.svg](det-area.svg): Unit square of area 1 and the parallelogram spanned by columns (3,1) and (1,2), which has area 5.
* [det-cases.svg](det-cases.svg): Three determinant cases: stretch with det 2 doubles area, swap with det -1 flips orientation, collapse with det 0 flattens the square to a line.
* [det-proof.svg](det-proof.svg): Parallelogram with corners (0,0), (3,1), (4,3), (1,2) inside a 4 by 3 box; six grey corner pieces of areas 1.5, 1, 1, 1.5, 1, 1 total 7, leaving area 5.
