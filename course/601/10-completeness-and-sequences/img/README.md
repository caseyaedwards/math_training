# Figures

Hand-authored SVG figures for [this lesson](../README.md). Each one is embedded in the lesson with a generator spec comment above it.

* [cobweb.svg](cobweb.svg): Cobweb picture of a(n+1) equals root of 2 plus a(n).
* [monotone-bounded.svg](monotone-bounded.svg): An increasing bounded sequence settles at its supremum.
* [rational-gap.svg](rational-gap.svg): Rational Newton steps closing in on root 2.
* [sequence-tube.svg](sequence-tube.svg): A sequence entering and staying in an epsilon tube.
* [supremum.svg](supremum.svg): Least upper bound of a set.
* [unique-limit.svg](unique-limit.svg): Two separate tubes around two candidate limits.
