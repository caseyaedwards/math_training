* [square-symmetries.svg](square-symmetries.svg): The eight symmetries of a square.
* [z4-cycle.svg](z4-cycle.svg): The turns of a square and clock arithmetic mod 4.
* [zero-divisors-z6.svg](zero-divisors-z6.svg): Multiplication table of Z6 with zero divisors.
