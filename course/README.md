# Course

Six levels. Each level assumes the ones before it. Finish the checkpoint at the end of a level before moving on.

| Level | Title | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| [101](101/README.md) | Foundations | Place value, the four operations, negatives, fractions, decimals, ratios, exponents, variables |
| [201](201/README.md) | Algebra I and Geometry | Linear equations, inequalities, functions, lines, systems, quadratics intro, Euclidean geometry, area and volume |
| [301](301/README.md) | Algebra II, Trig and Precalculus | Functions in depth, polynomials, exponentials and logarithms, trigonometry, conics, sequences and series, complex numbers |
| [401](401/README.md) | Calculus | Limits, derivatives, integrals, the Fundamental Theorem, series, multivariable intro |
| [501](501/README.md) | Linear Algebra, Probability and Discrete Math | Logic, counting, probability, distributions, graphs, vectors, matrices, eigenvalues |
| [601](601/README.md) | Proof and Higher Math | Proof techniques, cardinality, number theory, real analysis, abstract algebra, topology |

## Placement

Take a level checkpoint cold. Score 80% or better and you can skip that level. Skim the lessons for anything you missed.

## Pairs Well With

* [Tracks](../tracks/README.md) go deeper on one field after the course introduces it.
* [Workshops](../workshops/README.md) apply a level to a real problem. Each workshop lists the level it needs.
