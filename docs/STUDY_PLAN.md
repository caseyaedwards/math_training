# Study Plan

The default pace is three to five hours per week in sessions of 30 to 45 minutes. Adjust freely.

## A Session

1. Warm up for five minutes: redo one exercise from the last session without looking.
2. Read **The Big Idea** and study **Picture It** until you can redraw the diagram from memory.
3. Work the worked examples on paper before reading each line.
4. Do the **Try It** exercises. Check answers only after you finish all of them.
5. Log the session in the Obsidian vault. Add every mistake to the mistakes journal.

## A Week

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Three study days | One lesson each |
| One practice day | Redo missed exercises and the hardest example from the week |
| One fun day | A workshop step, a history chapter, or a track concept |

## Suggested Path

| Phase | Material | Approximate weeks at 4 hours per week |
|---|---|---|
| Refresh | [101](../course/101/README.md), placement through checkpoints | 2 to 4 |
| Core algebra and geometry | [201](../course/201/README.md) | 4 to 6 |
| Precalculus | [301](../course/301/README.md) | 5 to 7 |
| Calculus | [401](../course/401/README.md) | 6 to 8 |
| Linear algebra, probability, discrete | [501](../course/501/README.md) | 7 to 9 |
| Proof and higher math | [601](../course/601/README.md) | 6 to 8 |

Interleave one workshop per level. Each [workshop](../workshops/README.md) lists the level it needs.
Read the [History of Math](../history/README.md) chapter that matches the level you are on.

## Spaced Review

Set `next_review` on each concept card in the vault:

* one day after learning
* one week after that
* one month after that

If a review fails, reset the interval to one day.
