# Story of an Idea

The chapters of this history go era by era. These five essays go idea by idea. Each one follows a single concept from its first appearance to today, across every era and culture that touched it. Read them after a few chapters, or before, as a map of what is coming.

## The Essays

| Essay | The idea in one line | Eras it crosses |
|---|---|---|
| [The Story of Zero](zero.md) | From an empty column to a number to half of every computer | 02, 04, 05, 06, 07, 09 |
| [The Story of Pi](pi.md) | Squeezing one ratio from 3.16 to trillions of digits, then proving it never ends | 02, 03, 04, 07, 08, 09, 10 |
| [The Story of Infinity](infinity.md) | From Zeno's paradox to infinities of different sizes | 03, 07, 08, 09 |
| [The Story of Algebraic Notation](notation.md) | How algebra goes from sentences to symbols like =, x and f(x) | 02, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08 |
| [The Story of Proof](proof.md) | From recipes to axioms to machine-checked certainty, and its limits | 02, 03, 04, 05, 08, 09, 10 |
