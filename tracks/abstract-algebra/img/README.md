# Abstract Algebra Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Abstract Algebra Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Abstract Algebra Concept Map.
* [triangle-symmetry-group.svg](triangle-symmetry-group.svg): Symmetries of an equilateral triangle and their Cayley table.
* [clock-arithmetic.svg](clock-arithmetic.svg): Clock arithmetic: 9 plus 5 equals 2 mod 12.
* [square-symmetries.svg](square-symmetries.svg): The eight symmetries of a square: four rotations and four reflections.
* [z4-cayley-table.svg](z4-cayley-table.svg): Addition table of integers mod 4 with identity and inverses highlighted.
* [z12-generators.svg](z12-generators.svg): Stepping by 5 visits all 12 hours, stepping by 4 visits only 3.
* [permutation-wiring.svg](permutation-wiring.svg): A permutation drawn as wires from top row to bottom row.
* [cosets-z12.svg](cosets-z12.svg): The subgroup 0, 4, 8 and its cosets split Z/12 into four equal blocks.
* [homomorphism-mod3.svg](homomorphism-mod3.svg): The map n mod 3 sends integers to a 3-hour clock.
* [mod5-vs-mod6.svg](mod5-vs-mod6.svg): Multiplication tables mod 5 and mod 6.
* [burnside-necklaces.svg](burnside-necklaces.svg): The six distinct 4-bead necklaces with two colors up to rotation.
* [galois-field-lattice.svg](galois-field-lattice.svg): Lattice of fields between Q and Q of root 2 and root 3.
* [powers-of-3-mod-7.svg](powers-of-3-mod-7.svg): Powers of 3 modulo 7 cycle through all nonzero residues.
