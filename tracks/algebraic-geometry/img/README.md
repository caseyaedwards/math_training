# Algebraic Geometry Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Algebraic Geometry Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear. Curves are traced from their equations by a marching-squares pass, not drawn by eye.

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Algebraic Geometry Concept Map.
* [chord-addition.svg](chord-addition.svg): Adding P and Q on an elliptic curve with a chord.
* [circle-zero-set.svg](circle-zero-set.svg): The circle as the zero set of a polynomial, with test points.
* [four-conics.svg](four-conics.svg): Ellipse, parabola, hyperbola and two lines.
* [cone-slices.svg](cone-slices.svg): Conics as slices of a double cone.
* [rational-circle.svg](rational-circle.svg): Lines through (minus 1, 0) find rational points on the circle.
* [four-cubics.svg](four-cubics.svg): Four cubic curves, including a node and a cusp.
* [line-meets-circle.svg](line-meets-circle.svg): A line meets a circle in 2, 1 or 0 real points.
* [projective-plane.svg](projective-plane.svg): Railway tracks meeting at the horizon, and lines through the origin.
* [bezout-count.svg](bezout-count.svg): Two conics meeting in 4 points and a cubic meeting a line in 3.
* [elliptic-torus.svg](elliptic-torus.svg): The real points of an elliptic curve as two loops on a torus.
* [tangent-doubling.svg](tangent-doubling.svg): Doubling a point with the tangent line.
* [curve-mod-29.svg](curve-mod-29.svg): The points of y squared equals x cubed minus x plus 1 mod 29.
* [sphere-meets-plane.svg](sphere-meets-plane.svg): A sphere and a plane meet in a circle.
