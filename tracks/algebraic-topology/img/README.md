# Algebraic Topology Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Algebraic Topology Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Algebraic Topology Concept Map.
* [sphere-vs-torus-loops.svg](sphere-vs-torus-loops.svg): Loops shrink on a sphere and get stuck on a torus.
* [count-the-holes.svg](count-the-holes.svg): Shapes with zero, one and two holes.
* [euler-characteristic.svg](euler-characteristic.svg): V minus E plus F for a cube, a tetrahedron and a picture-frame torus.
* [simplices.svg](simplices.svg): The 0-, 1-, 2- and 3-simplex.
* [loop-shrinks-or-sticks.svg](loop-shrinks-or-sticks.svg): A loop shrinks in the plane and catches on a hole.
* [winding-numbers.svg](winding-numbers.svg): Loops with winding numbers 0, 1, 2 and minus 1.
* [torus-from-square.svg](torus-from-square.svg): A torus as a square with opposite edges glued.
* [betti-numbers.svg](betti-numbers.svg): Betti numbers of a point, a circle, a sphere and a torus.
* [hollow-vs-filled.svg](hollow-vs-filled.svg): A hollow triangle has a hole, a filled one does not.
* [brouwer-fixed-point.svg](brouwer-fixed-point.svg): Brouwer fixed point in one dimension and a map in a room.
* [hairy-ball.svg](hairy-ball.svg): A combed sphere needs a bald spot, a combed torus does not.
* [tda-barcode.svg](tda-barcode.svg): Persistent homology of a noisy ring with its barcode.
