# Complex Analysis Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Complex Analysis Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Complex Analysis Concept Map.
* [multiply-by-i-rotation.svg](multiply-by-i-rotation.svg): Multiplying by i rotates a point 90 degrees in the complex plane.
* [i-quarter-turn.svg](i-quarter-turn.svg): Multiplying by i is a quarter turn: 1, i, -1, -i.
* [complex-plane-point.svg](complex-plane-point.svg): The point 3 + 2i, its conjugate and its modulus.
* [polar-multiplication.svg](polar-multiplication.svg): Multiplying complex numbers multiplies lengths and adds angles.
* [euler-formula-circle.svg](euler-formula-circle.svg): Unit circle showing e to the i theta equals cos theta plus i sin theta.
* [roots-of-unity.svg](roots-of-unity.svg): The six sixth roots of unity on the unit circle.
* [z-squared-map.svg](z-squared-map.svg): The map z squared doubles angles and squares lengths.
* [derivative-rotate-scale.svg](derivative-rotate-scale.svg): Near a point an analytic map rotates and scales tiny squares.
* [power-series-spiral.svg](power-series-spiral.svg): Partial sums of the series for e to the i pi spiral to minus one.
* [conformal-grid.svg](conformal-grid.svg): Image of a square grid under z squared: parabolas meeting at right angles.
* [contour-integrals.svg](contour-integrals.svg): Two circular contours, one around the singularity of 1/z and one not.
* [residue-semicircle.svg](residue-semicircle.svg): Semicircle contour enclosing the pole at i for 1/(1+z^2).
* [zeta-critical-strip.svg](zeta-critical-strip.svg): Critical strip of the Riemann zeta function with the first zeros.
