# Differential Equations Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Differential Equations Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Differential Equations Concept Map.
* [logistic-slope-field.svg](logistic-slope-field.svg): Slope field of the logistic equation with solution curves.
* [rate-equals-rule.svg](rate-equals-rule.svg): Curve y = e^(0.5t) with tangent slopes equal to half the height.
* [slope-field-t-minus-y.svg](slope-field-t-minus-y.svg): Slope field of dy/dt = t - y with solution curves.
* [euler-method-steps.svg](euler-method-steps.svg): Euler method steps versus the true curve for dy/dt = y.
* [growth-and-decay.svg](growth-and-decay.svg): Exponential growth and decay curves with doubling and half-life marked.
* [logistic-phase-line.svg](logistic-phase-line.svg): Rate f(y) = y(1 - y) plotted against y with a phase line.
* [newton-cooling.svg](newton-cooling.svg): Newton cooling of a server from 90 to room temperature 20.
* [damping-three-cases.svg](damping-three-cases.svg): Underdamped, critically damped and overdamped oscillators.
* [phase-plane-spiral.svg](phase-plane-spiral.svg): Vector field and spiral trajectory for x' = -x - 2y, y' = 2x - y.
* [laplace-detour.svg](laplace-detour.svg): Laplace transform turns a differential equation into algebra.
* [heat-equation-profiles.svg](heat-equation-profiles.svg): Temperature profiles of the heat equation at three times.
* [lorenz-attractor.svg](lorenz-attractor.svg): Lorenz attractor projected onto the x-z plane.
