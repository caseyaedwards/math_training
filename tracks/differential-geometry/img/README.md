# Differential Geometry Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Differential Geometry Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Differential Geometry Concept Map.
* [three-curvatures.svg](three-curvatures.svg): Sphere, cylinder and saddle with their principal directions.
* [curve-velocity.svg](curve-velocity.svg): Position and velocity on a circle.
* [curvature-circles.svg](curvature-circles.svg): Circles of radius 1, 2 and 4 and their curvatures.
* [osculating-circles.svg](osculating-circles.svg): Best-fit circles on the parabola y = x squared.
* [helix-frame.svg](helix-frame.svg): A helix with its tangent, normal and binormal.
* [tangent-plane.svg](tangent-plane.svg): Grid lines, tangent plane and normal on a sphere.
* [saddle-principal.svg](saddle-principal.svg): Principal curvatures of a saddle.
* [torus-curvature-signs.svg](torus-curvature-signs.svg): Where a torus has positive, zero and negative curvature.
* [pizza-slice.svg](pizza-slice.svg): A flat slice droops, a curled slice stands.
* [cylinder-vs-sphere.svg](cylinder-vs-sphere.svg): A cylinder unrolls flat, a sphere peel tears.
* [sphere-metric.svg](sphere-metric.svg): Equal coordinate cells at the equator and at 60 degrees north.
* [great-circle.svg](great-circle.svg): Great circle versus latitude route, on a globe and on a flat map.
* [sphere-triangle.svg](sphere-triangle.svg): A spherical triangle with three right angles.
* [circle-charts.svg](circle-charts.svg): Two charts that together cover a circle.
* [gps-clock-drift.svg](gps-clock-drift.svg): GPS clock drift from gravity and from speed.
