# Dynamical Systems and Chaos Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Dynamical Systems and Chaos Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear. Every curve, orbit and point set is computed from the real map, not sketched.

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Dynamical Systems and Chaos Concept Map.
* [bifurcation-diagram.svg](bifurcation-diagram.svg): Bifurcation diagram of the logistic map for r from 2.5 to 4.
* [iterate-cos-timeline.svg](iterate-cos-timeline.svg): Pressing cos again and again, and halving a debt each month.
* [fixed-points-diagonal.svg](fixed-points-diagonal.svg): Fixed points where the graph crosses the diagonal.
* [cobweb-staircase-and-spiral.svg](cobweb-staircase-and-spiral.svg): Cobweb plots for x/2 + 1 (staircase) and cos x (spiral).
* [derivative-zoom.svg](derivative-zoom.svg): Zooming in on cos x near 0.739 to show the slope as a local stretch factor.
* [stability-slope-test.svg](stability-slope-test.svg): Four linear maps with slopes 0.5, -0.5, 1.5 and -1.5 around the same fixed point.
* [retry-storm-gain.svg](retry-storm-gain.svg): Load under retry gains 0.5, 0.9 and 1.1.
* [logistic-orbits.svg](logistic-orbits.svg): Logistic map orbits for r = 2.8, 3.2, 3.5 and 3.9.
* [period-doubling.svg](period-doubling.svg): A 2-cycle cobweb at r = 3.2 and the shrinking gaps between period doublings.
* [autoscaler-oscillation.svg](autoscaler-oscillation.svg): Autoscaler replica counts with and without metric delay.
* [sensitive-dependence.svg](sensitive-dependence.svg): Two logistic orbits that start 0.0000000001 apart, and their gap on a log scale.
* [lorenz-attractor.svg](lorenz-attractor.svg): The Lorenz attractor in the x-z plane, and two nearby forecasts splitting.
* [koch-curve.svg](koch-curve.svg): Koch curve levels 0 to 3 and the Koch snowflake.
* [fractal-dimension.svg](fractal-dimension.svg): Similarity dimension of a line, a square and the Koch curve.
* [mandelbrot-set.svg](mandelbrot-set.svg): The Mandelbrot set by escape time, with marked points.
