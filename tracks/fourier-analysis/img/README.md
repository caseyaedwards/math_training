# Fourier Analysis Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Fourier Analysis Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Fourier Analysis Concept Map.
* [time-frequency-signature.svg](time-frequency-signature.svg): The same signal in the time domain and the frequency domain.
* [sine-wave-anatomy.svg](sine-wave-anatomy.svg): A sine wave: amplitude, frequency, period and phase.
* [wave-sum.svg](wave-sum.svg): Two sine waves and their sum.
* [square-wave-partial-sums.svg](square-wave-partial-sums.svg): Fourier partial sums approaching a square wave, with the Gibbs overshoot.
* [coefficient-by-multiplying.svg](coefficient-by-multiplying.svg): Multiply the signal by a test wave and find the area to get each coefficient.
* [square-wave-spectrum.svg](square-wave-spectrum.svg): Spectrum of a square wave: odd harmonics with sizes 4 over n pi.
* [seasonality-spectrum.svg](seasonality-spectrum.svg): Four weeks of hourly request rates and their spectrum with daily and weekly peaks.
* [rotating-phasor.svg](rotating-phasor.svg): A point turning on the unit circle; its shadows are cosine and sine.
* [transform-pairs-width.svg](transform-pairs-width.svg): A narrow pulse has a wide spectrum and a wide pulse has a narrow spectrum.
* [dft-roots-of-unity.svg](dft-roots-of-unity.svg): The eight eighth roots of unity used by an 8-point DFT.
* [fft-butterfly.svg](fft-butterfly.svg): The FFT butterfly network for 8 points: three stages of pairwise combine steps.
* [convolution-moving-average.svg](convolution-moving-average.svg): A moving average is a convolution that smooths a noisy series.
* [convolution-theorem.svg](convolution-theorem.svg): The convolution theorem: convolution in time equals multiplication in frequency.
* [low-pass-filter.svg](low-pass-filter.svg): A low-pass filter removes a 40 Hz hum from a 3 Hz signal.
* [aliasing.svg](aliasing.svg): A 9 Hz wave sampled 10 times per second looks like a 1 Hz wave.
* [haar-wavelet.svg](haar-wavelet.svg): Haar wavelet transform of eight numbers into averages and details.
* [time-frequency-tiles.svg](time-frequency-tiles.svg): How Fourier, short-time Fourier and wavelets split time and frequency.
