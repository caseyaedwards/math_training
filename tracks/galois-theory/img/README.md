# Galois Theory Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Galois Theory Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Galois Theory Concept Map.
* [galois-correspondence.svg](galois-correspondence.svg): Field lattice of the field with root 2 and root 3, matched with the upside-down subgroup lattice.
* [quadratic-parabola.svg](quadratic-parabola.svg): The parabola y = x² - 2x - 3 crossing zero at -1 and 3.
* [history-timeline.svg](history-timeline.svg): From Babylon to Galois and Wantzel.
* [cubic-bombelli.svg](cubic-bombelli.svg): The Bombelli cubic with three real roots and a complex detour.
* [root-swap.svg](root-swap.svg): Swapping the two roots of x² - 2 keeps every rational fact.
* [nested-fields.svg](nested-fields.svg): Fields nest inside each other, with sample members.
* [extension-basis.svg](extension-basis.svg): A field extension as a grid of rational coordinates, with a table of degrees.
* [tower-law.svg](tower-law.svg): Two towers of fields where degrees multiply.
* [galois-group-klein.svg](galois-group-klein.svg): The four symmetries of the field with root 2 and root 3, and their composition table.
* [roots-of-unity.svg](roots-of-unity.svg): Fifth roots of unity on the circle, moved by z to z².
* [s3-lattice.svg](s3-lattice.svg): The splitting field of x³ - 2 and the subgroups of S3.
* [derived-series.svg](derived-series.svg): Derived series of S3, S4 and S5.
* [quintic-three-roots.svg](quintic-three-roots.svg): The quintic x⁵ - 4x + 2 with three real roots.
* [constructible-degrees.svg](constructible-degrees.svg): Ruler and compass reach only degrees that are powers of 2.
* [cube-and-angle.svg](cube-and-angle.svg): Doubling the cube and trisecting 60 degrees.

The coordinates come from a small generator script kept outside the repository. Every curve point and every circle position is computed, not placed by eye.
