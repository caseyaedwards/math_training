# Knot Theory Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Knot Theory Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear. The knot pictures are drawn from 3D parametric curves, and the generator checked each one by computing its Jones polynomial from the drawn crossings.

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Knot Theory Concept Map.
* [three-knots.svg](three-knots.svg): The unknot, the trefoil and the figure-eight knot.
* [tie-and-glue.svg](tie-and-glue.svg): An overhand knot on a string, then glued into a trefoil.
* [crossing-anatomy.svg](crossing-anatomy.svg): A shadow crossing and the same crossing with over/under information.
* [reidemeister-moves.svg](reidemeister-moves.svg): The three Reidemeister moves.
* [unknot-three-diagrams.svg](unknot-three-diagrams.svg): Three diagrams of the unknot with 2, 1 and 0 crossings.
* [tricolor-trefoil.svg](tricolor-trefoil.svg): A tricoloring of the trefoil and the crossing rule.
* [prime-knot-counts.svg](prime-knot-counts.svg): Number of prime knots for crossing numbers 3 to 10.
* [crossing-signs.svg](crossing-signs.svg): Positive and negative oriented crossings.
* [three-links.svg](three-links.svg): The Hopf link, the unlink and the Borromean rings.
* [knot-sums.svg](knot-sums.svg): The granny knot and the square knot as connected sums.
* [crossing-change.svg](crossing-change.svg): Switching one crossing of the trefoil gives the unknot.
* [kauffman-smoothing.svg](kauffman-smoothing.svg): The A- and B-smoothings of a crossing.
* [dna-strand-passage.svg](dna-strand-passage.svg): A topoisomerase strand passage changes one crossing.
