# Measure Theory Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Measure Theory Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Measure Theory Concept Map.
* [riemann-vs-lebesgue.svg](riemann-vs-lebesgue.svg): Riemann slices vertically, Lebesgue slices horizontally.
* [measure-additivity.svg](measure-additivity.svg): Length and area add over pieces that do not overlap.
* [counting-measure.svg](counting-measure.svg): Counting measure and probability weights on the faces of a die.
* [rationals-cover.svg](rationals-cover.svg): Covering the rationals in 0 to 1 with intervals of shrinking length.
* [outer-measure-cover.svg](outer-measure-cover.svg): Outer measure as the smallest total length of a cover.
* [vitali-circle.svg](vitali-circle.svg): Rotated copies of a Vitali set fill the circle.
* [sigma-algebra-rules.svg](sigma-algebra-rules.svg): Sigma-algebra rules: complements, countable unions, and the sets a partition generates.
* [continuity-from-below.svg](continuity-from-below.svg): Growing nested intervals have lengths that climb to the length of their union.
* [cantor-set.svg](cantor-set.svg): Cantor set construction removing middle thirds.
* [simple-function-approx.svg](simple-function-approx.svg): Simple functions approximate x squared from below.
* [coins-sorting.svg](coins-sorting.svg): Counting coins one by one versus sorting them into piles by value.
* [dirichlet-function.svg](dirichlet-function.svg): The Dirichlet function: 1 on rationals and 0 on irrationals.
* [escaping-bump.svg](escaping-bump.svg): Tall thin bumps with area 1 that converge to zero pointwise.
* [probability-space.svg](probability-space.svg): Probability as area in the unit square.
