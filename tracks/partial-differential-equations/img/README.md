# Partial Differential Equations Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Partial Differential Equations Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Partial Differential Equations Concept Map.
* [heat-snapshots.svg](heat-snapshots.svg): A hot slab in a cold rod spreads out and fades over time.
* [partial-derivative-slices.svg](partial-derivative-slices.svg): A function of position and time with one slice at fixed time and one at fixed position.
* [random-walk-spread.svg](random-walk-spread.svg): Distribution of random walkers after 25, 100 and 400 steps.
* [curvature-heat.svg](curvature-heat.svg): Peaks cool and valleys warm: the heat equation moves temperature toward the neighbor average.
* [rod-boundary-conditions.svg](rod-boundary-conditions.svg): The same rod with both ends held cold, and with the right end insulated.
* [stencil.svg](stencil.svg): The explicit finite-difference stencil on a space-time grid.
* [stability-blowup.svg](stability-blowup.svg): Finite differences with r = 0.4 stay smooth and with r = 0.6 blow up.
* [heat-modes-decay.svg](heat-modes-decay.svg): Sine modes of the heat equation and how fast each one decays.
* [dalembert-split.svg](dalembert-split.svg): A bump on a string splits into two half-size bumps moving apart.
* [string-modes.svg](string-modes.svg): Standing waves on a string fixed at both ends: the first four modes.
* [laplace-plate.svg](laplace-plate.svg): Steady temperature in a square plate with the top edge at 100 and other edges at 0.
* [three-families.svg](three-families.svg): The heat, wave and Laplace equations side by side.
* [square-pulse-series.svg](square-pulse-series.svg): A square pulse built from sine modes, and the same sum after a short time.
* [cfl-cone.svg](cfl-cone.svg): The numerical domain of dependence must contain the physical one: the CFL condition.
