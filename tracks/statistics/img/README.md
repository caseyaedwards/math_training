# Statistics Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Statistics Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Statistics Concept Map.
* [bell-curve-sigma.svg](bell-curve-sigma.svg): Normal curve with one, two and three sigma bands.
* [mean-vs-median.svg](mean-vs-median.svg): Dot plot of five latencies with mean and median marked.
* [latency-histogram-percentiles.svg](latency-histogram-percentiles.svg): Right-skewed latency histogram with p50 and p99.
* [same-mean-different-spread.svg](same-mean-different-spread.svg): Two data sets with the same mean and different spread.
* [z-score-ruler.svg](z-score-ruler.svg): Ruler converting latency to z-scores.
* [clt-dice-means.svg](clt-dice-means.svg): Distribution of the mean of n dice for n = 1, 2, 5.
* [confidence-intervals.svg](confidence-intervals.svg): Twenty 95 percent confidence intervals around a true mean.
* [p-value-tail.svg](p-value-tail.svg): Null distribution with the tail beyond the observed statistic shaded.
* [regression-residuals.svg](regression-residuals.svg): Scatter plot with least squares line and residuals.
* [ewma-anomaly.svg](ewma-anomaly.svg): Metric with daily wave, EWMA baseline and one anomaly.
* [bootstrap-schematic.svg](bootstrap-schematic.svg): Bootstrap: resample the data many times and look at the spread.
* [bayesian-update.svg](bayesian-update.svg): Prior, likelihood and posterior for a coin bias.
* [multiple-comparisons.svg](multiple-comparisons.svg): Twenty p-values with one below 0.05 by chance.
