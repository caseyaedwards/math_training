# Stochastic Processes and Queueing Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Stochastic Processes and Queueing Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Stochastic Processes and Queueing Concept Map.
* [latency-hockey-stick.svg](latency-hockey-stick.svg): M/M/1 response time versus utilization, the signature diagram.
* [random-walk.svg](random-walk.svg): A 20-step random walk with the square-root envelope.
* [markov-up-down.svg](markov-up-down.svg): Two-state Markov chain for a server that fails and gets repaired.
* [markov-steady-state.svg](markov-steady-state.svg): Convergence to the steady state from two different starts.
* [poisson-arrivals.svg](poisson-arrivals.svg): Poisson arrivals on a timeline and the distribution of counts per second.
* [queue-anatomy.svg](queue-anatomy.svg): Arrivals, a waiting line and servers, with Kendall notation.
* [littles-law.svg](littles-law.svg): Little's law as one area counted two ways.
* [birth-death-chain.svg](birth-death-chain.svg): Birth-death chain with a balance cut.
* [mm1-queue-length.svg](mm1-queue-length.svg): M/M/1 queue length distributions at 50% and 90% utilization.
* [slowdown-bars.svg](slowdown-bars.svg): Slowdown factor at six utilizations.
* [pooling-three-designs.svg](pooling-three-designs.svg): Two separate lines, one shared line and one fast server.
* [erlang-c-curves.svg](erlang-c-curves.svg): Erlang C probability of waiting for 1 to 16 servers.
* [kingman-variability.svg](kingman-variability.svg): Kingman mean wait for three levels of service variability.
* [tail-latency.svg](tail-latency.svg): M/M/1 response time distribution with p50, mean and p99.
* [fanout-tail.svg](fanout-tail.svg): Probability that a fan-out request hits a slow backend.

The diagrams are generated from computed coordinates, which keeps every curve and bar to scale.
