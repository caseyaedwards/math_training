# Topology Diagrams

Hand-authored SVG diagrams for the [Topology Track](../README.md), listed in the order they appear:

* [concept-map.svg](concept-map.svg): Topology Concept Map.
* [holes-classification.svg](holes-classification.svg): Shapes and capital letters grouped by number of holes.
* [rubber-sheet-shapes.svg](rubber-sheet-shapes.svg): Circle, square and triangle are the same to a topologist; a figure eight is not.
* [euler-characteristic-solids.svg](euler-characteristic-solids.svg): Tetrahedron and cube flattened into planar graphs with V minus E plus F equal to 2.
* [gluing-diagrams.svg](gluing-diagrams.svg): Gluing diagrams for a cylinder, a Mobius strip and a torus.
* [triangle-inequality.svg](triangle-inequality.svg): Triangle inequality for a metric.
* [open-vs-closed-disk.svg](open-vs-closed-disk.svg): Open disk with dashed boundary versus closed disk with solid boundary.
* [stereographic-projection.svg](stereographic-projection.svg): Stereographic projection maps a circle minus one point onto a line.
* [connected-and-compact.svg](connected-and-compact.svg): Connected versus disconnected sets and compact versus non-compact intervals.
* [classification-of-surfaces.svg](classification-of-surfaces.svg): Sphere, torus and double torus with genus and Euler characteristic.
* [fundamental-group-loops.svg](fundamental-group-loops.svg): Loops in the punctured plane: one shrinks to a point, one winds around the hole.
* [unknot-and-trefoil.svg](unknot-and-trefoil.svg): An unknot and a trefoil knot diagram.
* [tda-circle-barcode.svg](tda-circle-barcode.svg): Points sampled near a circle with growing balls and a persistence barcode.
