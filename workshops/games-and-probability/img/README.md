# Images

* `dice-grid.svg`: a six by six grid of two-dice sums with the six cells that sum to 7 highlighted along the diagonal.
* `running-average.svg`: the running average of 10,000 seeded die rolls (Go `math/rand/v2` PCG, seed 42, the same rolls as `code/dice`) on a log x axis, settling near the dashed line at 3.5.
* `monty-hall-tree.svg`: the Monty Hall probability tree for a player who picks door 1, with branch probabilities and stay versus switch outcomes totalling 1/3 and 2/3.
* `birthday-curve.svg`: the exact probability of a shared birthday for groups of 0 to 80 people, crossing 50% at 23 and reaching 0.9901 at 57.
