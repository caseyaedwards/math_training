# Images

* [vector-arrow-list.svg](vector-arrow-list.svg): the vector (3, 1) as an arrow and as a list, and (3, 1) + (1, 2) = (4, 3) tip to tail.
* [grid-shear.svg](grid-shear.svg): unit grid before and after the shear [[1, 1], [0, 1]], with i-hat and j-hat showing what the matrix columns mean.
* [four-transforms.svg](four-transforms.svg): the house under scale, rotate 90, shear, and reflect, with where i-hat and j-hat land in each.
* [compose-order.svg](compose-order.svg): the house under rotate-then-scale (S R) and scale-then-rotate (R S), showing that order matters.
* [house-rotate-30.svg](house-rotate-30.svg): a house polygon and its copy rotated 30 degrees about the origin, every new corner labelled.
* [rotate-two-ways.svg](rotate-two-ways.svg): rotating (2, 2) by 30 degrees as an angle change and as 2 of column 1 plus 2 of column 2.
* [homogeneous-translate.svg](homogeneous-translate.svg): the house rotated 30 degrees and then shifted by (3, 1) with one 3x3 matrix.
* [vector-angle.svg](vector-angle.svg): vectors a = (3, 1) and b = (1, 2) with the 45 degree angle between them and the cosine working.
* [dot-projection.svg](dot-projection.svg): the dot product as the length of a times the shadow of b on a.
* [similarity-heatmap.svg](similarity-heatmap.svg): cosine similarity heat map of the hand-made 4-dimensional word embedding.
* [embedding-slice.svg](embedding-slice.svg): king, queen, man, woman on the royalty and gender axes, with king - man + woman landing beside queen.
* [regression-line.svg](regression-line.svg): five request-rate versus CPU points, the least-squares line CPU = 9.7 x + 10.9, and dashed residuals.
* [residual-bars.svg](residual-bars.svg): the five residuals of the CPU fit as bars above and below zero.
