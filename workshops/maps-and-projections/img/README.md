# Images

* [globe-great-circle.svg](globe-great-circle.svg): orthographic globe with latitude and longitude lines every 15 degrees, the JFK to LHR great circle in orange, and a dashed evenly interpolated lat/lon line for contrast.
* [sphere-to-xyz.svg](sphere-to-xyz.svg): side view and top view right triangles that turn latitude φ and longitude λ into x = cos φ cos λ, y = cos φ sin λ, z = sin φ.
* [mercator-vs-equirect.svg](mercator-vs-equirect.svg): equirectangular and Mercator grids at the same scale, with computed latitude-line spacing and equal-ground Tissot circles at 0, ±30, and ±60 degrees.
* [route-on-mercator.svg](route-on-mercator.svg): the JFK to LHR great circle on a Mercator map, arching to about 53.6 degrees north, next to the straight rhumb line.
