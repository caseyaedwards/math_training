# Images

* `piano-log-axis.svg`: three octaves of piano keys from A2 to A5 above a log frequency axis (110, 220, 440, 880 Hz equally spaced under the A keys) and a linear axis (the same frequencies crowded to the left).
* `dft-spectrum.svg`: the naive DFT amplitude spectrum of a noisy mix of 440, 660, and 1,320 Hz from the Go program, with the three peaks marked.
* `sine-anatomy.svg`: a 440 Hz sine wave with its period (2.273 ms) and amplitude marked.
* `octave-waves.svg`: a 220 Hz wave (two cycles) above a 440 Hz wave (four cycles), with every low peak meeting a high peak.
* `fifth-waves.svg`: a 440 Hz wave (two cycles) above a 660 Hz wave (three cycles), with dashed lines where their peaks line up every 1/220 s.
* `semitone-staircase.svg`: bars for the 13 equal-tempered notes from A4 to A5, with the first and last steps in hertz labeled.
* `cents-ruler.svg`: a 0 to 1,200 cent ruler with equal-tempered ticks and the simple ratios marked as dots.
* `harmonics-string.svg`: a string vibrating in its first five modes, from 110 Hz to 550 Hz.
* `square-partial-sums.svg`: a dashed square wave with its Fourier partial sums for 1, 3, and 5 odd harmonics.
* `aliasing.svg`: a 60 s cycle sampled every 10 s (follows the signal) and every 50 s (traces a false 300 s wave).
* `seasonality.svg`: an hourly metric over 14 days from the Go program, and its DFT spectrum with the weekly (bin 2) and daily (bin 14) peaks.
