# Images

* `nines-ladder.svg`: a ladder of six SLO targets from 99% to 99.999%, each with a log-scale bar of allowed downtime per 365-day year.
* `request-budget-grid.svg`: 1,000 squares with one orange square, the error budget of a 99.9% request SLO.
* `budget-burn-down.svg`: error budget remaining over a 30-day window for burn rates of 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 6x, and 14.4x.
* `multiwindow-alert.svg`: a 3% error incident over 240 minutes with the raw rate, the 5-minute and 1-hour averages, the 1.44% threshold, and the page window where both averages are above it.
* `series-vs-parallel.svg`: block diagrams of two 99% components in series (0.9801) and in parallel (0.9999).
* `replicas-nines.svg`: bars of the number of nines for one to four independent 99% replicas.
* `correlated-zone.svg`: two replicas in parallel inside one shared availability zone, with the naive and the true availability.
* `at-least-one-page.svg`: curves of the chance of at least one page across a 168-hour week for four hourly alert chances.
